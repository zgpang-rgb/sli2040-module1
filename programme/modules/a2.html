Training Leadership — The Five Sections

The five sections of a training-leadership build, how to tell which one a friction actually belongs to, and why Act sits at stage five of the short-cycle loop rather than stage one.

Learning outcomes

After this module a learner should be able to:

  1. Distinguish the five sections of a training-leadership build: problem and Operating Environment, instructional leadership, research methodology, findings and discussion, conclusion and contribution.
  2. Identify which section a training-school friction actually belongs to before proposing a solution.
  3. Order the six short-cycle stages (Notice, Locate, Evidence, Test, Act, Compare) and explain why Act is stage five rather than stage one.
  4. Judge whether a claimed finding is carried by the methodology presented for it.
  5. Produce an Official Closed capstone brief walking a live friction from their own school through all six stages.
