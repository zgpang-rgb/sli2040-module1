Research Methodologies

Designing an evaluation that will carry the claim made from it — and reading someone else's report closely enough to tell whether theirs does.

Learning outcomes

After this module a learner should be able to:

  1. Design a defensible evaluation for a training question.
  2. Separate descriptive claims about people measured from inferential claims about people not measured.
  3. Interrogate a contractor or school evaluation report against the evidence it actually presents.
  4. Spot overclaimed causation, a missing sample size, or an R-squared quoted without context.
  5. Decide which error they can least afford in the decision at hand.
  6. Report results as estimate, interval and n, with the boundary of the claim stated alongside.
