Workplace Developmental Conversations

Four stances — coach, mentor, advisor, counsellor — sorted by who owns the agenda and who owns the answer, and what to do when a conversation drifts or reaches the edge of scope.

Learning outcomes

After this module a learner should be able to:

  1. Sort the four stances (coach, mentor, advisor, counsellor) by who owns the agenda and who owns the answer.
  2. Diagnose whether a performance gap is knowledge, skill, clarity or motivation, and read competence against commitment.
  3. Notice stance drift in a live conversation and apply recover, declared switch, or notice-and-hold.
  4. Distinguish powerful from leading questions, and reflection from interrogation.
  5. Use experience as one data point when mentoring, avoiding the 'when I was in your appointment' trap.
  6. Recognise referral signals and apply the five-step response at the boundary of scope.
  7. Produce an eight-step worked plan for a real developmental conversation.
