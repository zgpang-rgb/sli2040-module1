Learning outcomes

  1. Place a live project within the SLI 2040 enablers, domains and thrusts, and the MLS Framework 2040 Military Learning Pillars.
  2. Route a solution-first request to the correct section using the architect / materials engineer / construction crew division of labour.
  3. Sort incoming signals into the Learning Science and Learning Technology watch areas.
  4. Diagnose a project stalled between Run and Measure in the Plan-Run-Measure operating loop.
  5. Work the short-cycle loop from problem immersion through controlled trial to validated scale.
  6. Review a draft Trial Implementation Plan against its five components and identify what to return first.
  7. Walk a live project through all six loop stages in the capstone.

Learning outcomes

  1. Distinguish the five sections of a training-leadership build: problem and Operating Environment, instructional leadership, research methodology, findings and discussion, conclusion and contribution.
  2. Identify which section a training-school friction actually belongs to before proposing a solution.
  3. Order the six short-cycle stages (Notice, Locate, Evidence, Test, Act, Compare) and explain why Act is stage five rather than stage one.
  4. Judge whether a claimed finding is carried by the methodology presented for it.
  5. Produce an Official Closed capstone brief walking a live friction from their own school through all six stages.

Learning outcomes

  1. Design a defensible evaluation for a training question.
  2. Separate descriptive claims about people measured from inferential claims about people not measured.
  3. Interrogate a contractor or school evaluation report against the evidence it actually presents.
  4. Spot overclaimed causation, a missing sample size, or an R-squared quoted without context.
  5. Decide which error they can least afford in the decision at hand.
  6. Report results as estimate, interval and n, with the boundary of the claim stated alongside.

Learning outcomes

  1. Sort the four stances (coach, mentor, advisor, counsellor) by who owns the agenda and who owns the answer.
  2. Diagnose whether a performance gap is knowledge, skill, clarity or motivation, and read competence against commitment.
  3. Notice stance drift in a live conversation and apply recover, declared switch, or notice-and-hold.
  4. Distinguish powerful from leading questions, and reflection from interrogation.
  5. Use experience as one data point when mentoring, avoiding the 'when I was in your appointment' trap.
  6. Recognise referral signals and apply the five-step response at the boundary of scope.
  7. Produce an eight-step worked plan for a real developmental conversation.
